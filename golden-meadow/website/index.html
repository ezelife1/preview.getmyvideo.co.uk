THE PROPERTY

Built for gathering, designed for escape

Golden Meadow is an architecturally imagined house of sculptural lines, combining steel, glass and stone with inspired outdoor space on one of the most beautiful stretches of the Devon coastline.

Designed to host large groups of friends and family, the house offers plush interiors with panoramic sea views, a cinema room, wood burner, dedicated workspace and Mediterranean-style flagstone terraces. A separate self-contained one-bedroom studio - The Workshop - adds extra privacy.

A CLOSER LOOK

A cinema room and a wood burner

Evenings at Golden Meadow revolve around the cinema room with its large Smart TV and comfortable seating, or the wood burner in the entrance hall - gathering spaces made for a celebration home.
